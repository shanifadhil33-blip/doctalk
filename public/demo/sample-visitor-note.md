# Visitor desk note

Northwind Studio, 120 Sample Avenue, Portland, OR 97204. This note is the current desk copy. Priya Shah, office coordinator, wrote it on March 18, 2026, and it replaces the printout dated January 9, 2026. If the two copies disagree, follow this one. The note is for the person covering the lobby desk, and for a host who is about to bring someone into the building. It is a sample. The names, the phone numbers, and the fees in it are fictional.

The building has a lobby, a loading dock, a staff garage, and three floors of offices. Visitors stop in the lobby. They do not go upstairs alone. The desk keeps a paper log, a box of blue lanyards, a box of yellow contractor badges, and a receipt book in the second drawer.

# Who this note is for

This note is for the weekday desk and for anyone who hosts a guest. Elena Voss is the studio director. Chris Patel from Harbor Supplies visits often and already knows the lobby, but he still signs the log. A new host should read the badge section and the third-floor section before the guest arrives.

The note does not train the night guard. The night guard has a separate sheet in the guard office. If you are covering the desk after 5:30 p.m., call the guard rather than guessing.

# Desk hours

The lobby desk is open on weekdays from 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Pacific Time. It is closed on Saturday and Sunday. On a weekday the first task at 8:30 a.m. is to unlock the visitor door, turn on the lobby lights, and put the log book on the counter. The last task at 5:30 p.m. is to lock the visitor door, leave the log in the second drawer, and tell the night guard that the desk is closed.

The desk is also closed all day on Friday, July 3, 2026. It reopens on Monday, July 6, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. A visitor who arrives on July 3 finds a sign on the door and does not get a badge that day. Mail that arrives on July 3 stays in the dock cage until Monday morning.

If you are ill, call Priya Shah before 8:00 a.m. so the backup cover can open the door on time. The backup cover this spring is Jordan Hale. Jordan has a desk key. Do not leave the key under the mat.

# Visitor badges

A visitor wears a blue lanyard. The badge is written by hand with the visitor's name, the host's name, and the date. It expires at 6:00 p.m. on the same day. A badge from yesterday is not valid, even if the visitor is the same person. Collect it and write a new one.

The host meets the visitor in the lobby within 10 minutes of the visitor signing the log. If the host is not there after 10 minutes, call the host once. If there is still no answer after another 5 minutes, ask the visitor to wait or to reschedule. Do not send the visitor upstairs to look for the host.

The visitor signs the paper log with three items: their name, their company, and the host's name. The log stays at the desk. It is not scanned at the end of the day. Priya Shah files a finished log book in the cabinet when the pages run out. The current book was started on February 2, 2026.

A visitor who refuses to sign the log does not get a badge. Offer them a chair and call the host. The host can come down. The visitor still does not go up without a signature.

# Parking

Visitors park in the surface lot on Sample Avenue, in stalls V1 through V8. Those eight stalls are marked with a blue V. The staff garage under the building is not for visitors. A visitor who pulls into the garage should be asked, politely, to move to the surface lot. The garage gate does not open for a visitor badge.

A visitor car that is still in stalls V1 through V8 after 7:00 p.m. is noted by the night guard. It is not towed on the same night. The guard writes the plate on the night sheet. Priya Shah calls the host the next weekday morning. Towing, if it is ever needed, is a separate decision and is not authorized by this note.

There is no visitor parking fee. Do not collect money for a stall. The lost-badge fee in the next sections is the only cash the desk handles.

# Where visitors wait

Visitors wait in the lobby chairs by the north window, to the left of the desk as you face the street. The chairs fit four people. If a fifth person arrives, offer the bench along the east wall. Do not send a group into the kitchen to wait, and do not send anyone to the third floor alone.

The lobby has a water cooler. Cups are in the cabinet under the cooler. There is no visitor coffee in the lobby. Coffee is in the kitchen, and only a hosted visitor goes there, with the host.

Children are uncommon. If a visitor arrives with a child, the child stays with the visitor. The desk does not watch the child. Say that plainly and kindly.

# Deliveries

Deliveries use loading dock B, on the alley side of the building. Dock B is open from 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. on weekdays. The courier cutoff is 3:30 p.m. A courier who arrives at 3:40 p.m. can still leave a package if the dock door is open, but the desk does not promise a same-day walk-up to the recipient after 3:30 p.m. Tell the courier the package will be walked up the next weekday morning.

Packages for Harbor Supplies go in Chris Patel's bin, which is the gray bin labeled HS on the dock shelf. They do not stay at the front desk overnight. If Chris is out, the bin is still the right place. Write the date on the packing slip.

A food delivery for a person in the building is not a dock delivery. The driver comes to the lobby. Call the person. The driver does not get a badge and does not go upstairs. If the person does not come down within 10 minutes, the driver keeps the order. The desk does not pay for it.

Large furniture and crates belong on dock B even if the vendor walks up to the lobby first. Walk them back to the dock. The lobby floor was refinished on February 20, 2026, and crates scratch it.

# Lost badges

A lost visitor badge costs 25.00 USD for the first loss in a calendar month. The visitor or the host may pay. The desk takes cash or the office card that lives in the locked tin. Write a receipt from the book in the second drawer. The receipt needs the date, the amount, the name on the badge, and your initials.

A second lost badge for the same person in the same calendar month costs 40.00 USD. Count the month by the date on the old receipt, not by the visitor's story. If you cannot find a receipt, charge 25.00 USD and leave a note for Priya Shah.

Do not take a personal card, and do not invoice the visitor's company from the desk. The 25.00 USD and the 40.00 USD are the only badge fees. There is no fee for a badge that is returned bent or faded. Swap it and write a new one at no charge.

A lost yellow contractor badge uses the same fees. Tell Elena Voss the same day if the lost badge was a contractor badge, because the contractor may still be on the list for tomorrow.

# Third floor rooms

The third floor has three rooms the desk books.

Cedar holds 6 people. It has a table and a phone. It does not have a camera.

Alder holds 10 people. It is the room contractors use when they need a table for plans. The May painting crew uses Alder.

Fir holds 14 people. Fir is the only room with a whiteboard camera. If someone asks for a room where a remote colleague can see the board, book Fir. Cedar and Alder cannot do that.

Book a room through the desk book, not by leaving a note on the door. A booking that ends after 4:00 p.m. needs Priya Shah's initials in the margin. A booking that ends at 4:00 p.m. or earlier does not. The rooms are not booked on Saturday or Sunday, and they are not booked on Friday, July 3, 2026.

If two hosts want Fir on the same afternoon, the earlier entry in the book wins. Do not split the room. Offer Alder to the later host.

# Kitchen

The shared kitchen is on the second floor. Peanut snacks are not allowed there. Priya Shah posted that rule on March 3, 2026, after a visitor had a reaction in the kitchen on February 27, 2026. The rule covers peanuts, peanut butter, and candy with peanuts. Other food is allowed. If you find a peanut snack, throw it out and leave a short note for Priya. Do not put it back in a drawer.

The fridge is cleared every Friday at 4:00 p.m. A labeled lunch that is still there at 4:00 p.m. on Friday is thrown out. Labels do not save it. Tell people this on Thursday if they ask. There is no exception for a lunch labeled with a Monday date.

Coffee is for staff and for a visitor who is with a host. A visitor sitting alone in the lobby does not get the kitchen code and does not go up for coffee. The host can bring a cup down.

The kitchen code is not written in this note. Hosts already have it. If a host has forgotten it, they call Priya Shah. The desk does not read the code aloud to a visitor.

Then call Priya Shah at extension 214. If the desk phone is already behind you, the cell phone is fine. Extension 214 rings Priya's desk and her cell.

The meeting point is the parking row in front of stalls V1 through V4, on Sample Avenue. Do not use the staff garage as a meeting point. Do not stay in the lobby to finish the log. The log can wait.

If a visitor is hurt but the building is safe, call 911 first if they want an ambulance, and then call Priya Shah at extension 214.
